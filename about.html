About WanderLux

A small team of travel lovers helping Australians see the world their way.

Our story

Built on one simple idea

WanderLux was founded in Parramatta in 2026 by a group of travel consultants who were tired of selling the same fixed packages to every customer. We believed that a family of five, a couple on their honeymoon and a solo backpacker should never get the same plan.

Today we design personalised holidays, guided tours and custom packages for destinations across Australia, Asia, Europe and the Pacific. Every booking is handled by one consultant from the first chat to the day you fly home.
